## 1. Platform Purpose

**Beyond Governance Theatre** is an empirical AI governance audit and regulatory technology (RegTech) platform designed to detect and remediate **governance theatre**—the structural decoupling between formal, symbolic AI policies and substantive technical, risk, and human-oversight controls.

Built on a **24-indicator psychometric measurement rubric**, the platform evaluates corporate annual reports, AI charters, and institutional disclosures against Indonesian statutory frameworks (**UU PDP No. 27/2022**, **SE Menkominfo No. 9/2023**, **OJK AI Governance**) and international standards (**ISO/IEC 42001:2023**, **NIST AI RMF 1.0**, **EU AI Act**, **OECD AI Principles**).

---

## 2. Core Objectives

1. **Detect Symbolic vs. Substantive AI Governance:** Quantify the gap between declared policy commitments (`IV1`) and verified technical safeguards (`IV2`), risk management (`IV3`), and organizational enablers (`IV4`).
2. **Evaluate Organizational AI Incident Exposure (`M1–M3`):** Assess documented harm severity, stakeholder reach, and organizational consequences on the 0–3 OECD incident scale.
3. **Forecast Audited Organizational Performance (`DV1–DV5`):** Link substantive AI governance to verified financial, legal/compliance, cybersecurity, operational, and reputational outcomes.
4. **Eliminate Disclosure Shadow Penalties:** Apply strict non-imputation rules (`NA ≠ 0`) for unverified public disclosures and provide a **90-Day Safe-Harbor Cure Window** for supervisory compliance.
5. **Deliver Actionable Remediation:** Generate a prioritized **30-60-90 Day Remediation Roadmap** and **Fix vs. Do Nothing** trajectory directly from uploaded organizational PDFs.

## 3. Repository Structure & Platform Modules

- **`apps/web/`**: Next.js interactive audit dashboard featuring:
  1. **AI Health Check**: Live 16-control + 3-incident scoring, local PDF Annual Report scanner, 5-dimension `DV1–DV5` performance forecast, and 30-60-90 day remediation planner.
  2. **Regulator Suite**: Jurisdiction-aware supervisory triage and Safe-Harbor cure rules (`Indonesia` & `International`).
  3. **Benchmarks**: Aggregated cross-sector empirical baselines (**Doctoral Proposal Feasibility Stage, N = 99** [14 Indonesia + 85 International]; expanding to **N = 198** in the Dissertation Stage).
  4. **Criteria & Standards**: Complete operational definitions and direct regulatory crosswalks across `IV1.1–IV4.4`, `M1–M3`, and `DV1_FIP–DV5_RVP`.
  5. **Docs & IP**: Standard operating procedure, intellectual property notice, and citation details.
  6. **Access & Billing**: Community/Academic, Enterprise Pro, and Regulator/Government deployment tiers.
  7. **Contact**: Direct academic correspondence, ORCID, and repository links.
- **`packages/rubric-engine/`**: Core deterministic scoring, decoupling detection, and regulatory mapping engine.

---

## 4. Data Governance & Confidentiality

- **Public Repository Scope:** This repository contains only the open-source psychometric rubric engine, web application interface, statutory reference links, and aggregated sector benchmarks (`N = 99` Doctoral Proposal Feasibility Stage).
- **Strict Quarantine of Raw Empirical Data:** Raw organization-level annual reports, internal AI charters, and unredacted coding workbooks are strictly quarantined locally (`confidential-data/`, excluded via `.gitignore`) and are **never** uploaded to or downloadable from GitHub.
