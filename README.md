## 1. Platform Purpose & Objectives

**Beyond Governance Theatre** is an empirical AI governance audit and regulatory technology (RegTech) platform grounded in the **Technology–Organization–Environment (TOE)** framework and **Socio-Technical Systems (STS)** theory. Built on a **24-indicator psychometric measurement rubric** (`IV1.1–IV4.4`, `M1–M3`, `DV1_FIP–DV5_RVP`), the platform evaluates organizational disclosures against Indonesian statutory mandates (**UU PDP No. 27/2022**, **SE Menkominfo No. 9/2023**, **OJK AI Governance**) and international standards (**ISO/IEC 42001:2023**, **NIST AI RMF 1.0**, **EU AI Act**, **OECD AI Principles**).

1. **Detect Symbolic vs. Substantive AI Governance:** Quantify decoupling between declared policy commitments (`IV1`) and technical risk controls (`IV2`), board/audit governance (`IV3`), and socio-technical enablers (`IV4`).
2. **Assess Organizational AI Incident Exposure (`M1–M3`):** Measure documented harm severity, stakeholder reach, and organizational consequences on the 0–3 OECD scale.
3. **Forecast Audited Organizational Performance (`DV1–DV5`):** Link substantive governance to audited financial, legal/compliance, cybersecurity, operational, and reputational outcomes.
4. **Eliminate Disclosure Shadow Penalties:** Enforce strict non-imputation (`NA ≠ 0`) for unverified filings and provide a **90-Day Safe-Harbor Cure Window** for supervisory compliance.
5. **Deliver Actionable Remediation:** Generate a prioritized **30-60-90 Day Remediation Roadmap** and **Fix vs. Do Nothing** trajectory directly from uploaded organizational PDFs.

## 2. Repository Structure & Platform Modules

- **`apps/web/`**: Next.js interactive audit dashboard:
  1. **AI Health Check**: Live 16-control + 3-incident scoring, local PDF Annual Report scanner, 5-dimension `DV1–DV5` performance forecast, and 30-60-90 day remediation planner.
  2. **Regulator Suite**: Multi-jurisdictional supervisory triage (`Indonesia` & `International` across 5 global regulatory archetypes) and Safe-Harbor cure rules.
  3. **Benchmarks**: Aggregated cross-sector empirical baselines from the **Dissertation Proposal (DP) Feasibility Cohort (N = 99: 14 Indonesia + 85 International)**.
  4. **Criteria & Standards**: Operational definitions and direct statutory crosswalks across all 24 indicators.
  5. **Docs & IP**: Standard operating procedure, psychometric validation summary (JASP v0.19), and citation details.
  6. **Access & Billing**: Community/Academic, Enterprise Pro, and Regulator/Government deployment tiers.
  7. **Contact**: Direct academic correspondence, ORCID, and repository links.
- **`packages/rubric-engine/`**: Core deterministic scoring, decoupling detection, and regulatory mapping engine.

## 3. Research Roadmap & Data Governance

- **Stage 1 (Current — Dissertation Proposal [DP] Feasibility Release, N = 99):** Full-stack web application (`Next.js` + `TypeScript` + deterministic 24-indicator rubric engine), local PDF Annual Report scanner, and aggregated empirical sector benchmarks from the **N = 99 DP Feasibility Cohort** (14 Indonesia + 85 International; `KMO = 0.84`, `ω = 0.78–0.87`). *Raw organization-level archival records and PDFs remain strictly quarantined locally (`confidential-data/`, excluded via `.gitignore`) and are never uploaded to GitHub.*
- **Stage 2 (Next — Full Dissertation Empirical Release, N = 198):** Expansion to the **1:1 stratified matched case–control dataset (N = 198: 99 incident-exposed + 99 matched non-incident organizations)** following Dissertation Proposal defense approval, updating platform benchmarks and structural mediation estimates (`P1–P5` and `P6a–P6d`).
